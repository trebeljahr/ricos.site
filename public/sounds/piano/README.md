# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, licensed under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Source of these MP3 conversions: <https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/>
(the Tone.js audio repository), downloaded 2026-11-09.

One sample every minor third from A0 to C8 (30 files, one velocity layer).
`src/lib/piano/sampler.ts` pitch-shifts the nearest sample to fill the notes in
between. The `/piano` demo pages credit the samples under each demo.
