# Semiconductor photograph assets

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 21 October 2026. Images are stored locally so the lesson does not depend on third-party image requests.

- `first-transistor-bell-labs.jpg`: Windell Oskay, *The First Transistor ever made, built in 1947 - Bell Labs*, photographed 1 October 2012. [Source and attribution](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_First_Transistor_ever_made,_built_in_1947_-_Bell_Labs.jpg). [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). Original photograph, unchanged. This is a modern photograph of the historical design on display; no claim is made that the photograph dates from 1947.
- `bc547-transistor.jpg`: Suyash Dwivedi, *BC547 Transistor - 01*, photographed 15 December 2025. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BC547_Transistor_-_01.jpg). [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Original photograph stored unchanged. The displayed photograph is cropped by its SVG viewport and annotated with collector, base and emitter labels; this labeled photographic view is also offered under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The labeled package and historical connection diagrams are separate schematic drawings. The BC547 lead order is checked against the [onsemi BC546–BC550 datasheet](https://www.onsemi.com/download/data-sheet/pdf/bc550-d.pdf), front view with the flat face toward the reader and leads downward: collector, base, emitter. This order is not universal to TO-92 packages.
